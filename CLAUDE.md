# Snail

Read [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) for the contributor contract and [GOAL.md](GOAL.md) for the active delivery ledger.

Single Worker serves React assets and API; D1 stores library metadata, uploads, devices and audit events; private R2 stores video and poster objects. The OpenCLI Connector runs locally and only sends approved metadata/media. Production is https://snail.hexly.ai. Brand assets are published independently by Hexly.

Version source: package.json. Research baseline: docs/01–11. Primary Codex is the only writer during initial delivery.
